# Privacy Policy for QuikAlpha

**Effective Date:** July 24, 2026

## Zero Data Collection
QuikAlpha was built with privacy as a fundamental principle. The application does not collect, store, share, or transmit any personally identifiable information (PII), financial data, or usage metrics. 

## No Accounts or Tracking
You are not required to create an account to use QuikAlpha. We do not use third-party analytics trackers, advertising networks, or crash-reporting tools that harvest your personal data. Your stock lookups, watchlists, and application activity remain entirely local to your device.

## Subscriptions and Purchases
If you choose to purchase a subscription, the transaction is processed securely through Apple's App Store. QuikAlpha does not have access to your payment information, billing address, or Apple ID. Apple handles all payment processing and receipt management.

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or the application, please reach out via email at: <quikalpha.support@gmail.com>
